About Me

Dr. Mohammad Lutful Hakim is an academic researcher working in applied electromagnetics, metamaterials, frequency selective surfaces, antennas, microwave and terahertz sensing, and AI-assisted electromagnetic design.

His research emphasizes compact and high-performance electromagnetic structures for wireless communication, electromagnetic interference shielding, sensing, and next-generation intelligent RF and terahertz systems.
